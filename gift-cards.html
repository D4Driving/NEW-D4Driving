Gift card terms

  • Valid for 12 months from the date of purchase.
  • Lesson and block gift cards always cover the hours they name, even if prices rise before they're used.
  • Any-amount gift cards are worth the amount paid, spent at the prices on the day of each lesson.
  • Use with Robert or Rakesh, automatic or manual. No assessment lesson needed.
  • Transferable. The gift card can be passed to someone else, including any unused hours or balance. Just get in touch and we'll reissue it in their name, with the same expiry date.
  • Refunds: a full refund within 14 days of purchase if the gift card hasn't been used. After that it can't be exchanged for cash.

Questions

Can I give the gift card in person?

Yes. At checkout, put your own email address as the one to send the gift card to. Robert will email it to you as a PDF that you can print, wrap and hand over.

Are the lessons automatic or manual?

Either. Robert teaches automatic in an electric Toyota C-HR+ and manual in a Toyota Aygo X, and Rakesh teaches manual in a VW Golf. The person receiving the gift card chooses.

What do they need before their first lesson?

A provisional driving licence, and they must be at least 17. The gift card lasts 12 months, so if it's for a 17th birthday they have plenty of time to get their licence sorted.

What happens if lesson prices go up?

A lesson or block gift card always covers the hours it names, even if prices rise before it's used. An any-amount gift card is worth the amount paid, spent at the prices on the day of each lesson.

Not sure which to choose?

Ask Robert. He'll help you pick the right gift for where they are with their driving.
