Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions.

How many driving lessons do I need to pass?
The DVSA says learners who pass typically have around 45 hours of professional lessons, but it varies a lot from person to person. At D4Driving every new student starts with a 90-minute assessment lesson, after which you get a personalised pass plan with a realistic estimate for you. Our structured approach is a big part of why 83% of our students pass first time.
How much do driving lessons cost in Peterborough?
Single lessons are £42 per hour, with a 1.5-hour lesson at £60 and a 2-hour lesson at £80. New students start with a 90-minute assessment lesson for £45 (one per student). Block bookings bring the rate down: 5 hours for £200, 10 hours for £390, 15 hours for £560, or 20 hours for £740 — that's just £37 per hour. A semi-intensive course (21.5 hours plus full test prep) is £840, and 4-hour test preparation sessions in Peterborough, Grantham or Kettering are £160. See the full breakdown in our prices section.
Do you offer manual and automatic lessons?
Yes, both. Robert teaches manual in a Toyota Aygo X and automatic in a Toyota Yaris Cross. Rakesh teaches manual in a VW Golf. All cars are modern and fitted with dual controls for your safety.
What areas do you cover?
We teach across Peterborough and surrounding areas including Hampton, Werrington, the Ortons, Stanground, Yaxley and Whittlesey. We also offer driving test preparation for the Grantham and Kettering test centres.
Are your instructors DVSA approved?
Yes. Robert is a DVSA Approved Driving Instructor (ADI) and has been teaching in Peterborough since 2017. Rakesh is also a fully qualified DVSA Approved Driving Instructor (ADI).
What is your first-time pass rate?
83% of D4Driving students pass their driving test on the first attempt — well above the national average of around 48%. We've helped 430+ local learners pass, and we hold a 5.0-star rating on Google.
How do I book a driving lesson?
The fastest way is to book online — you'll see real availability and get instant confirmation. You can also message us on WhatsApp or call/text 07872 347686.
Is the free mock driving test really free?
Yes — completely free. In exchange, you agree to the session being filmed and published on our YouTube channel, where 25,000+ subscribers learn from real mock tests. Sessions run in Peterborough on selected Mondays with limited slots. Learn more and apply here.
