(a)More than 72 hours' notice: no charge.
(b)Between 48 and 72 hours' notice: 25% of the lesson fee.
(c)Between 24 and 48 hours' notice: 50% of the lesson fee.
(d)Less than 24 hours' notice: 100% of the lesson fee.
