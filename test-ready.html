Why tests go wrong

You've done the lessons. So why does test day still feel uncertain?

Most learners don't fail because they can't drive. They fail on the small things: a rushed look at a junction, a mirror check that comes a second too late, or a mind that goes blank when the examiner asks a "tell me" question.

Those things can be fixed with a bit of structure in the weeks before the test. This pack gives you that structure.

Questions

Good to know

Is this a physical product?

No. It's an instant PDF download. Print it at home, at a library or at a print shop. The revision cards print best on thicker paper or card.

Will it work if my instructor isn't D4Driving?

Yes. It covers the UK practical driving test, which is the same everywhere. Where methods vary by car, such as manoeuvre reference points, there's space to write in what your instructor teaches you.

Is it for manual or automatic?

Both. Almost everything applies to either, and where the technique differs, such as clutch control versus brake control in manoeuvres, the pack says so.

Will this guarantee I pass?

No one can honestly promise that. What the pack does is help you prepare properly, so you walk in knowing what to expect.

Is it endorsed by DVSA?

No. It's an independent guide written by a DVSA approved driving instructor. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by DVSA.

Can I share it with a friend?

Please don't share the file, but you can print as many copies as you need for your own household.
